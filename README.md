# Jane's Unique Gifts & Designs — website redesign concept

VVVIP redesign concept for Jane's Unique Gifts & Designs, a home-based gift shop in Chilliwack, BC, Canada (janesuniquegiftsanddesigns.ca).

- All 41 products with their exact names, prices, descriptions and original photographs, plus the original logo and black / taupe / cream brand colours.
- Home, Shop (search, filters, sort), product pages for every item, About, Testimonials, Markets and Contact.
- Cart, checkout and contact form are demo-only: they state plainly that nothing was ordered or sent.
- No announcement bar, newsletter, shipping/returns/privacy/terms pages or account system — the original site has none of these, so none were invented.

Built as a design concept for the business owner to review. Open `index.html` in a browser to view it.
